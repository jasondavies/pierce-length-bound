The candidate adjoins the positive integer rank-three Hankel invariant I3 to
the three gap coordinates. Its proposed degree gain is deg(graph) >= 5d/2 for
a nonexceptional component of degree d. Even degree excludes d=3, so after
conics are counted separately, d>=4 gives graph degree >=10. Applying the
coefficient-uniform affine-curve count in dimension four then gives the term
X^5 max(H,S)^(1/10), with S bounded by a constant times a/T^3.

This argument uses the ORDINARY pattern count. No weighted-lattice counting
estimate is needed for its improvement. The Lean assembly retains the already
proved divisor slack in the ordinary X^5 estimate, sufficient for an arbitrary
epsilon loss in the final exponent. The older lattice developments remain
available but are not analytic premises of the new reduction.

IntegralLiftInvariant:

- Identifies it with the rank-three Hankel invariant and proves I3>=1.
- Proves I3*q^3 <= 14*a*H^3 by an explicit Newton-basis Gram estimate.

    1 <= I3 <= floor(896*A^3*H^3/a^2) = selectedLiftHeight a A T.
  The harmless constant is larger than the candidate's sharper bound; the
  powers and the final exponent are unchanged.

IntegralLiftResolvent and IntegralLiftMoments:
- Prove the polynomial rank-three resolvent over any commutative ring.
- Instantiate it for actual orbit moments and the actual monic quartic:
    I3 * P(-q) = a * ((q-p3)*H3 - J).
- Prove the raw rank-three determinant formula, its cubic leading form and
  its elementary height bound in a positive gap box.
- Prove the mixed raw determinant formula and its quartic leading form.

IntegralLiftParabola:
- Proves that quadratic gap coordinates give mixed-minor degree at most eight
  and, with distinct leading nodes, rank-three determinant degree six.

  denominator degree twelve, arbitrary cancellation preserves degree difference
  three, so the reduced denominator is nonconstant. Constructing these data for
  every actual conic is still part of the assembly obligation below.

IntegralLiftCofactors, IntegralLiftMomentSystem and IntegralLiftCoordinateSystem:

  nu_j=c_j*(c1-c0)/(c1^2-c0*c2) from its explicit maximal-minor vector, with

- Construct the uniform polynomial matrix in the three free node coordinates,

  cofactor's multivariate total degree by 40.
- Construct rational polynomials orbitLiftNumeratorQ and orbitLiftDenominatorQ,

  belong to any rational component ideal containing that state. Positivity of
  I3 also proves that the numerator does not belong to that component ideal.

Shared obligations closed since the 180/611 candidate
---------------------------------------------------
The actual projective coordinate filtration, homogeneous infinity quotient,
finite chart section, and its Hilbert-polynomial dimension are constructed.
The local identity implies parity of the actual section dimension, proving
even degree for nonexceptional prime components over C and directly over Q.
The rational cover contains at most 24 components, each of degree two or even
degree at least four. Nonexceptional conics have a unique infinity point. The
actual conic's containing-plane restriction has zero leading discriminant and
nonzero square coefficient; these are derived from its prime ideal and actual
degree-two Hilbert polynomial, rather than assumed as a classification premise.
FixedDividendParabolicCharts handles both coordinate charts and proves that
one of their pivots is nonzero. The six actual coefficients have nonzero square
coefficient, zero discriminant and nonzero reconstruction denominator J, derived
from a rational point and primality. FixedDividendOrbitConicParameter now gives
a bounded integer quadratic representation of every integral component point
in an injective integer parameter. It uses actual minor labels bounded by X
and a containing integer plane bounded by L: with K=10(12X^4)^2 L^2, the common
nonzero denominator is at most 12LK^3, and numerator coefficients are at most
33LK^3. The existing bounded-plane theorem supplies the plane when three
bounded distinct integral component points exist; smaller sets are finite
exceptions.

FixedDividendOrbitConicIdentities proves the next actual-component bridge:
with at least 25 integral component points, the normalized quadratic
parametrization satisfies both defining equations as polynomial identities.
The degree-at-most-24 substitutions vanish at 25 distinct parameters. Their
leading coefficients then prove that all five leading nodes are distinct,
rawHankelThree has degree six, muH4 has degree twelve, and the mixed minor has
degree at most eight. No symbolic-equation or leading-node assumption is
supplied to the final actual-orbit wrapper. Sets with fewer than 25 points
are bounded exceptions. The quotient normalization and resolvent connections have since been proved.
IntegralLiftOrbitConicProperness gives the actual reduced denominator/numerator
classical degree difference three after at most 492 exceptional supported
points. IntegralLiftConicSlack proves the uniform slowly growing conic count,
including the actual plane, coefficient-height and integer-value arguments.

ExceptionalPlaneCount through ExceptionalSlack prove the complete exceptional
minor-pattern estimate O(X^4 log(2X)), including nonprimitive and zero cases,
the four exact exceptional relations and their integer coordinate changes.

ConicPlaneHeight proves a containing plane with normal bounded by 8H^2 and
constant by 24H^3, using three distinct bounded points. ConicParameterHeight
constructs a bounded-height integer quadratic parametrization once the actual
binary quadratic has zero discriminant and the required coefficients are
nonzero. ConicCofactors proves the kernel identities and determinant bounds.
ConicResultantHeight and ConicFactorHeight prove polynomial height bounds for
resultants and all integer factors, including after common-factor cancellation.
These ingredients do not yet constitute the actual conic counting theorem.

FixedDividendLatticeCoordinates and FixedDividendLatticeCurveCover also connect
the actual lattice coordinates to the rational component cover, preserving
Hilbert degree. This closes an older interface but is unnecessary for 151/513.
