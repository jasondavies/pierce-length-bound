Mathematical change
-------------------
The new graph has three original coordinates, three signed curvature minors W,
and the positive integer rank-five invariant I5=A D: ambient dimension seven.
The candidate claims degree at least 4d+1 on original nonexceptional components
of degree d>=4, hence at least 17. A sufficient upper bound is 247d<=5928.
Powered coordinates W_j^m and I5^n would give degree at least 10n+7m.

The ordinary O(X^5) pattern enumeration is retained. None of the four added
coordinates is enumerated as a new freely chosen pattern parameter. No weighted-
lattice estimate is used in the unconditional joint-lift proof.
