Mathematical assessment
-----------------------
The candidate's exponent calculation is consistent: the nonlinear contribution
has exponent 31/105, and the possible lines have exponent 13/44. Their ordering
and the saving 1/1672 over 45/152 are checked in Lean.

The new algebraic identities passed the finite exact checks described below.
No counterexample was found. These checks and the currently proved lemmas do
not constitute verification of the complete argument, the uniform estimates,
or a novelty/priority determination.

The cited counting statement was checked against Corollary 2.2 of Cluckers,
Debes, Hendel, Nguyen and Vermeulen (2025). It applies to affine curves
irreducible over Q, uniformly in their coefficients. The original statement
is Theorem 3 of Castryck, Cluckers, Dittmann and Nguyen (2020). For degrees
2 through 24 it implies the required square-root bound with logarithmic loss.

Lean components completed
--------------------------
All the following modules are imported by PierceLengthBound.lean. They contain
no sorry, custom axioms, or native_decide.

1. FixedDividendMoments.lean
   - Conversion from nu to mu by multiplication by X + q.
   - Weighted Newton-basis shift and nu(F_j) = b_(i+j)/a.
   - The first three raw moments after multiplication by F_j, with explicit
     support assumptions.
   - newtonQuartic_orthogonal: the four specified moment/row equations imply
     orthogonality to monomials of degree zero through three.
   This is a theorem about coefficients satisfying those equations. The new
   module does not yet derive all of them from the actual minor pattern.

2. FixedDividendResultant.lean
   - resultant_step and resultant_four prove the resultant product for
     polynomials satisfying the stated three-term recurrence.
   - hankel_ratio_product proves the telescoping determinant-ratio formula.
   - fixed_dividend_normalization proves the final scalar normalization.
   The application still requires proving the recurrence and determinant-ratio
   formulas for the Pierce moment functional. In particular the actual-orbit
   identity A Res(P,xR) = a D^4 has not yet been proved in Lean.

3. FixedDividend.lean
   - The normalized Newton coefficient signs and exceptional coefficient pairs.
   - The determinant of the quadratic leading form and its nonvanishing.
   - pattern_cubic_obstruction: the cubic leading form cannot be the quadratic
     times any linear form. This verifies the coefficient obstruction for all
     real coefficients with the required signs, not just sampled patterns.
   - cluster_sums_zero: the Vandermonde argument with repeated nodes.
   - five_cluster_pair: five nonzero weights with zero cluster sums and at
     least two clusters force a two-element zero-sum cluster.
   - line_direction_exceptional combines these facts under the stated leading
     moment and repeated-node hypotheses.
   - exceptional_minors verifies the four determinant transformations.
   Establishing these hypotheses for every line on f = Omega = 0 remains to
   be done; no geometric curve-counting theorem is asserted by this module.

4. FixedDividendExponent.lean
   - nonlinear_exponent: T^105 <= K C^11 D^21 a^31.
   - line_exponent: T^44 <= K C^5 D^9 a^13.
   - local_count_dichotomy combines the two contributions.
   These are implications from explicitly stated local count, gap, height and
   drop estimates. They do not assert those premises for Pierce orbits.

Axioms.lean audits six representative new results in addition to the eight
existing main theorems. The new results use only the standard axioms propext,
Classical.choice and Quot.sound (exceptional_minors needs no Classical.choice).

Reproducible exact checks
-------------------------
Run: python3 scripts/check-fixed-dividend.py
Requires SymPy; tested with version 1.14.0. The sampler is deterministic.

The script checks 202 starts, each with sixteen subsequent positive states:
positive probability weights, orthogonality of the quartic and the shifted
quintic, D = nu(P^2) > 0, the resultant normalization, and A P(-q) = a H_4.
It also checks seven degree-ten leading-form specializations and reproduces
the entire displayed normalization polynomial for a = 473 and equal gaps.
These are finite exact checks, not a proof of the general polynomial identity
or of an asymptotic estimate.

Remaining formal proof obligations
-----------------------------------
A. Construct the relevant orthogonal polynomials for the finite positive
   moment functional, prove their recurrence and Hankel-ratio identities,
   and apply FixedDividendResultant to the actual orbit polynomials.
B. Prove the resultant's degree-ten leading-form identity in the multivariate
   coefficient ring, including specialization and denominator clearing.
C. Prove absolute irreducibility of the affine quadratic from its rank-three
   leading part, transfer the cubic obstruction to polynomial nondivisibility,
   and establish coprimality with Omega. Formalize the curve decomposition
   and degree bound, and use the line-direction result on its line components.
D. Prove the exceptional-minor-pattern count, including nonprimitive vectors,
   bounded integral coordinate changes, and the dyadic primitive-normal sum.
   The existing total minor count with divisor loss already has sufficient
   subpower slack for the final exponent; removing that loss is not necessary.
E. Prove the external coefficient-uniform curve-counting theorem. Searches of
   the pinned Mathlib found no applicable integral-point counting result.
   The published proof of Theorem 3 uses the plane-curve estimate in Corollary
   4.2.4, Proposition 4.2.1 and its determinant-method input in Theorem 3.1.1,
   and the controlled projection in Proposition 4.3.1. These involve further
   arithmetic and algebraic-geometric ingredients, including Bezout bounds.
   These are unimplemented proof obligations, not assumptions licensed by
   the user, and must not be hidden in a local-count hypothesis.
F. Assemble the local orbit count, choose supported starts with sixteen future
   gaps, apply the existing minor-height and divisor estimates, absorb all
   logarithmic factors, and sum over the dyadic blocks with uniform constants.
G. Only after the resulting P_le_13_44 passes its axiom audit: update the main
   manuscript and PDF, independent Challenge, comparator, submission metadata,
   and complete submission verification.
